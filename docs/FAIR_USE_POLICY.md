# NEXMINT AI Fair Use Policy

NEXMINT AI uses paid AI providers, IPFS storage, RPC infrastructure, and queue workers. The credit system exists to keep the service stable and sustainable for creators.

## Fair Use Rules

- Do not attempt to bypass credit limits or plan limits.
- Do not automate abusive generation jobs or bot traffic.
- Do not create repeated failed jobs to exhaust provider quota.
- Do not use temporary AI provider URLs as permanent NFT metadata.
- Do not publish collections with broken metadata or invalid `baseURI` values.

## Provider Limits

AI providers can rate-limit, pause, or reject requests. When quota or rate limits are reached, NEXMINT may pause generation jobs instead of continuing to fail remaining items.

## Service Protection

NEXMINT may throttle generation, pause jobs, or require plan upgrades when usage risks harming service stability or causing abnormal operational cost.

## Marketplace and Launchpad

External marketplace links are free. Internal marketplace listing and launchpad publishing require eligible plans because they use additional indexing, verification, and infrastructure.
